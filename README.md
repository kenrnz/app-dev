# My Favorite Movie
## Favorite Movie: Fantastic Mr. Fox
**Description:** *Fantastic Mr. Fox* is a clever and adventurous fox who outsmarts three mean farmers-
Boggis, Bunce, Bean to steal food for his family. It's a charming story about wit, courage, and family,
written by Roald Dahl.

**Main Characters**

**Mr. Fox** - the clever protagonist

**Mrs. Fox** - supportive and caring

**Ash, Kristofferson** - Mr. and Mrs. Fox's kids

**Boggis, Bunce, Bean** - the greedy farmers
